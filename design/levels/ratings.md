# The levels' ratings board

After Retro Studios' whiteboard for Donkey Kong Country Returns
(`design/research/platformer-fun.md` §3): every level is marked, and the
weakest is reworked until all have ◎.

× not fun yet · △ works, but flat · ○ good · ◎ a level to be proud of

**These first marks are mine, from building and testing them. Nobody has
played either level in its current form: the creative lead's marks replace
mine.**

## What every level is checked against (from the research)
1. One idea, in four beats: shown safely, tested, twisted, finished.
2. Inside each beat a chain: no two challenges in a row the same; one rest.
3. Every required gap can be made with a plain jump; the stride is a bonus.
4. Resin never lies. Some of it is a dare.
5. Four letters (two on the line, two off), two bonus areas, one thorn knot.
6. Each secret has a tell, and the level's tells differ from each other.
7. One midway banner, before the hard half.
8. Something this level has that the last one didn't.
