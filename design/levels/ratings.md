# The levels' ratings board

After Retro Studios' whiteboard for Donkey Kong Country Returns
(`design/research/platformer-fun.md` §3): every level is marked, and the
weakest is reworked until all have ◎.

× not fun yet · △ works, but flat · ○ good · ◎ a level to be proud of

**These first marks are mine, from building and testing them. Nobody has
played either level in its current form: the creative lead's marks replace
mine.**

## What every level is checked against
The full list is `design/rulebook.md` §4.3 (shape, fairness, the hunt,
proof: nineteen points). In short:
1. One idea, in four beats; inside each beat a chain, no two challenges in
   a row alike, one rest.
2. A spine that never forks blind; ribs that rejoin ahead; one high road.
3. Every required gap, landing and wait is within the region's numbers,
   with the plain jump.
4. Every machine's path is in sight; every safe drop is 7 tiles or less or
   hung with Resin; one mistake costs one flame.
5. Four letters (two on the line, two off), two bonus areas, one knot;
   every secret has a tell seen from the spine, and the tells differ.
6. One banner, at the middle, before the hard half.
7. Something this level has that no level before it had.
8. Two scripted tours pass: the spine, and every secret.
