# 1-S The Hollow Oak (blockout, awaiting the creative lead's review)

Builder: `game/tools/build_l1_s.gd` → `game/scenes/levels/l1_s_hollow_oak.tscn`.
Pictures: `docs/levels/1-S/blockout/`.

**Region 1's secret level:** the map's grotto, opened by leaving 1-3
through its secret exit. A short, harder climb inside the Great Ironbark:
60 tiles wide and 70 high, two legs.

- **Harder than the region's rule, on purpose:** gaps of 5, landings of 3,
  and two ledge catches (4 up). A fall never kills: she lands on the wood
  below.
- **Leg 1**, going right: a root, then shelves of bark. **The midway
  banner** near its top. A step against the east wall, an island back.
- **Leg 2**, going left, to the hollow at the top: **a great chest** (25
  Resin) and the way out.
- **The great secret:** everyone climbs. The knot sleeps in the far corner
  of the oak's floor, past the first root, where nothing leads.
- No letters and no bonus areas: the level is the bonus.

**Not built:** the design gives each secret level an extra lantern flame
as its reward. Only the chest is there.

## Validation (in engine)
`room=l1_s`: `mode=tour` **ALL PASS** (both legs, the banner, the way out);
`mode=secrets` **ALL PASS** (the knot in the far corner, the great chest).

- **The reward:** the extra flame needs building; is it the right prize?
