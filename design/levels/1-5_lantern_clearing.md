## Secrets
- **The great secret: Morvath's thorn knot.** The crate is there for the
  rat. Carried past the rat and thrown at the ruin block's cracked mouth,
  it opens the hollow where the knot sleeps. (A crate comes back only
  when she does.)
- **Behind the lift:** the wall's foot is a false wall (motes drift into
  it): **letter L** and **bonus 1** (pillars, 12 s).
- **Letter E:** over the second floating island, a jump on the spot.
- **Bonus 2, a drop** (blocks, 16 s): a low stone under the last islands,
  a bead on it. The way back up is a ledge catch (4 up).
- **Chests (2):** on the wall, by the gate.
