# Lost Keep: Platformer — the lore bible

Everything the game knows about its world, in one place. It gathers what
was spread across `CLAUDE.md`, `design/game-structure.md`,
`design/regions/2_hearth_plains.md`, `design/enemies.md`,
`design/rulebook.md` and Lost Keep's own `design/world-and-regions.md`
(the earlier game this one grew from). **Nothing here is new invention:**
where the sources disagree or leave a gap, this page says so rather than
choosing.

How sure each statement is:

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| (none) | **Canon**: the creative lead's, in `CLAUDE.md`'s lore section or decided in review |
| **[planned]** | In the approved game plan (`game-structure.md`, region plans), not yet in the game |
| **[proposed]** | A suggestion in the design docs, never confirmed by the creative lead |
| **[Lost Keep]** | From the earlier game's notes; not yet placed in this game |
| **[open]** | Undecided, or the sources disagree |

When a decision changes, change this page in the same commit as the doc it
came from.

## 1. The world: Vaelora

- **Vaelora** is a world with **three moons**. When they align, it is a
  **Triad Eclipse**, and the eclipses frame its history. (In the game: the
  Triad sky with its small moons hangs over region 1's backdrops.)
- **The Sovereign Prism** is Vaelora's living crystalline heart and its life
  source: the bond between its people and the natural order. It is the
  game's last place.

### The shape of the world [Lost Keep]
Lost Keep's world map gave the directions a meaning: **north is Vexari
power** (the Spire), **south is the source** (the Prism), and the Spire,
the Refuge, the Vats and the Prism stand in one column between them, "the
Tether made physical". **West is living nature** (Fen-Stalker, Deep-Glow,
Tide-Weaver); **east is industry and fire** (Dusk-Drifter, the Carapax
Foundry). This game's regions run in a line instead (§6), but the meaning
of north, south, east and west can still guide where things stand.

## 2. The mythic past

1. **The Oru'Vex**, radiant extradimensional architects, came down during a
   Triad Eclipse and raised **the Spire of the Sovereign Prism** around it.
2. **The Schism of Light.** Seeking perfection, they tried to burn away
   their flaws in the Prism's core. The Prism fractured, and each of them
   was cleaved into **two tethered beings**:
   - the **Vexari**: rapacious, avian-mantis;
   - the **Orun**: stoic, six-limbed.

   Each Vexari is still linked to one Orun.
3. **Morvath the World-Root**, the thorny, one-eyed embodiment of Vaelora's
   crust and stars, withdrew to her **Observatory** and entrusted the world
   to the **Lirien**.

The floating **Oru'Vex stones** of the High-Spire (region 6) are the
architects' remains. [planned]

## 3. The peoples

### The Lirien
Graceful, wing-cloaked mortals in **seven Kindreds**, all of one ancestry
and all able to **meld memories**. Their wings lie **dormant**: the
Vexari's harvesting drains them. (Kleo's short cloak, lined in red, is her
folded wings.)

### The Seven Kindreds

| Kindred | People | What they are | In this game |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High-Spire** | Aetheri | Scholars. Their **High Voice** leads all the Voices and is manipulated by the Vexari. Vesper's people | Region 6 [planned] |
| **Ironbark** | Vanguard | Martial guardians. **Kleo's people**; home of the Lost Keep | Region 1 |
| **Deep-Glow** | Lumen | Cave-dwellers who make their own light. Sora's people | No region (see §6) |
| **Tide-Weaver** | Nautil | Water folk | No region |
| **Fen-Stalker** | Mire-Kin | Swamp hunters of the Thornveil forest, scorned as "primitive" (by the High-Spire [Lost Keep]) | Region 4 [planned] |
| **Hearth-Plains** | Loam-Born | Farmers and silk-growers, neighbours of the Rootlings | Region 2 (2-2 built as a blockout) |
| **Dusk-Drifter** | Ash-Walker | Wanderers of the ash wastes, masters of fire | Region 3 [planned] |

Each Kindred is governed by a **Voice** (a Sovereign Matriarch); every
Voice answers to the **High Voice**. **Never call the Silk Mother a
Matriarch:** that word belongs to the Voices.

### The Manufactured Divide
The Vexari stoke pride and prejudice between the Kindreds so they never
unite, and never notice their villagers vanishing. **Kleo starts
distrusted outside Ironbark.** The Hearth-Plains distrust Ironbark in
particular; the Ironbark Guard has been pitted against them. [Lost Keep]

How the Divide was meant to play out [Lost Keep; not placed in this game]:
Kleo is met coldly in the Hearth-Plains and with contempt in the
High-Spire; each Voice is won by rescuing their people or bringing proof
through the memory-meld; **uniting the Voices** opens the way to the
Prism. **The High Voice** is the last to turn: a believer, not a villain,
fought or convinced depending on the proof Kleo has.

### The Rootlings
Small, round, cheerful humanoids who farm, cook, sing and dance in
close-knit villages: the most innocent people in Vaelora, which is why
their harvesting must land as horror.
- **Kleo never kills a Rootling, and a Rootling is never an enemy.**
  Infected Rootlings may appear in cocoons or vats, never as a foe.
  [Lost Keep]
- An empty Rootling village tells the horror without words: the cooking
  pots still hot, a song half-written. [Lost Keep] Region 2's empty farms
  carry this now (§6, region 2).
- At the Refuge, rescued Rootlings run **the kitchen** and garden: the
  hub's heart. [planned]

### The Vexari
Decaying lords who rule from the Spire, revered as **"the Shepherds of the
Prism"**. They are its harvesters. Each is tethered to an Orun.

### The Orun
The Vexari's tethered other halves: stoic, six-limbed. **[open]** No role
in this game yet. (Lost Keep planned an optional Orun Sanctum with a
"tether choice" and an Orun guardian.)

## 4. The crime: Anima and the Blight-Tide

1. **The Alchemist**, a Vexari, experimented on the Prism and discovered
   **essence extraction**.
2. The Vexari harvest **Anima**, pure resonant life-force, from captive
   Lirien and Rootlings in subterranean **vats**, and consume it to stay
   young.
3. The abuse destabilises the Prism, and the **Blight-Tide** seeps out of
   it: plants twist, animals mutate and turn aggressive, creatures are
   infected, Lirien included.
4. The Vexari try to **weaponise** the Blight. It threatens everyone, the
   Vexari too.
5. They build the **Carapax**, shell-forged automatons, to purge
   rebellion.

**The missing** are in **cocoons**, in **vats**, or **turned by the
Blight**. Most can be rescued, not all: some have turned, and that is where
the grief comes from. [Lost Keep]

**Where the Blight comes from shows:** its crimson veins radiate out of
the Prism. [Lost Keep] In this game the first crimson veins appear in
Thornveil (region 4), where Kleo finds the Blight's source growing.
[planned]

Lost Keep's ideas that do **not** fit this game as it stands: the Blight
spreading by act and changing old regions (there is no backtracking here),
and Kleo building up Blight exposure as a status.

## 5. The characters

### Kleo
A young **Lirien of the Ironbark Guard**, in short chibi proportions: a
big rounded head about half her height, short legs, plain solid dark
vertical-rectangle eyes. Navy hair in a long high ponytail; a small brown
leather jerkin with bronze fastenings; a teal neck accent and a gold
collar clasp; a short dark cloak lined in red (her folded, dormant
wings); a short silver sword in her right hand; a brass lantern at her
left hip. She casts **teal Anima magic**. Later she wears a wide-brimmed
hat and a long coat (sold by the Refuge's tailor [planned]).

- **Her voice** is wordless: a "hut" as she swings, grunts as she climbs
  and throws.
- **Her lantern** lights dark corners and pulses near hidden letters and
  bonus entrances. [planned]
- **Her letters, K-L-E-O,** spell her name in every level.

### Allies
- **Sora** (Deep-Glow): among the missing, freed mid-game. With no
  Deep-Glow region in this game she is **rescued in Thornveil** (region 4).
  [planned]
- **Vesper** (High-Spire): among the missing. Freeing Vesper triggers the
  forbidden **memory-meld**: **the Reveal** (region 6). [planned]
- **The Ironbark smith**: held in the Silk Mother's great cocoon; freed, he
  makes Kleo's next gift (Barkgrip). [planned; the gift is open, §7]
- **Her captain**: the Ironbark Ward was "her captain's shield", taken
  back from the fallen warden. [planned] Who the captain is, and whether
  the warden *was* the captain, is **[open]**.

### Morvath the World-Root
Thorny, one-eyed, the embodiment of Vaelora's crust and stars; she
watches from her Observatory and **tests Kleo** as her mentor.
- **Her thorn knots** (pale bark, an amber-gold eye; never red) sleep in
  every level, one each: the level's great secret. Kleo wakes them.
- **The knots have been watching since region 1:** Morvath meets Kleo at
  the Carapax Foundry's broken gate (region 5). [planned, from the old
  ability plan, where she gave Kleo the Thorn-Line there]
- **What waking every knot does is [open].** One proposal: each region's
  seven knots wake one of seven seals on **Morvath's Observatory**, the
  game's last and hardest stops, after the ending. [proposed, rulebook E7]

### The Alchemist
The Vexari who discovered essence extraction; everything (the vats, the
Blight, the weaponised Tide) starts with them.
- **Glimpsed early** (notes; a figure behind glass at the Foundry), fought
  last, at the Anima Vats. [Lost Keep]
- **The Alchemist's notes** are a lore trail; the Refuge's archivist keeps
  them. [planned]

### The bosses, region by region

| Region | Boss | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Keep's fallen warden** | An armoured giant on a throne (Ironbark). **Not designed yet**: his attacks, how he is hurt and his phases await the creative lead |
| 2 | **The Silk Mother** | The Hearth-Plains' great silk-spinner, a beloved, almost domestic beast, twisted by the Blight. She cocoons villagers, Lirien and Rootlings, "to keep them safe". **Her cocoons began as a way to hide the villagers from the Vexari harvesters;** the Blight turned protection into imprisonment [Lost Keep]. **A victim, not just a monster.** Never "the Spider Matriarch" |
| 3 | **The Cinder Warden** | (Dusk-Drifter) Nothing more written |
| 4 | **The Thornveil Reaper** | (Fen-Stalker) A skeleton fused into a tree, burning with stolen Anima. Its stolen Anima flows back into Kleo and wakes her wings [planned] |
| 5 | **The Carapax Prototype** | (The Foundry) The Vexari's war-machine |
| 6 | **A Vexari Shepherd** | (The Spire) One of the harvesters themselves |
| 7 | **The Alchemist** | (The Anima Vats) Where it began |

## 6. The story, region by region

People keep disappearing from the Ironbark Guard's post. Kleo goes looking.
Each boss frees the captives held there, and they walk into the Refuge.

The order is lively to grave, bright to dark: four open-air regions, then
under the Vexari's roofs. [planned]

| # | Region | The place | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flooded Sanctuary** (first called the Ironbark Wilds) | Ironbark's forest: great trees, ruin stone, flooded ruins, the Guard's road to the Keep | Kleo leaves home on the trail of the missing; the Keep's fallen warden at its gate |
| 2 | **The Hearth-Plains** | Golden fields at late afternoon, silk orchards, windmills, Rootling villages, hay barns: **and nobody in them** | The Silk Mother is cocooning the farmers. Its dread is emptiness, not the dark |
| 3 | **The Dusk-Drifter Wastes** | Ash dunes and glass flats under a violet dusk, nomad caravans, bone-white ruins | Kleo crosses the wastes to win the Ash-Walkers' fire |
| 4 | **Thornveil** | A swamp of giant roots and stilt huts, glowing fungi; **the first crimson Blight veins** | She finds the Blight's source growing; Sora is freed |
| 5 | **The Carapax Foundry** | Vexari war-works on a volcanic shelf: smokestacks, forges, brass and soot | She breaks into the works that make the Blight weapons; Morvath meets her at the broken gate |
| 6 | **The High-Spire** | Ivory towers above the clouds, Oru'Vex stones, the Scribes' libraries; the Vexari's Spire in sight | The High Voice is deceived; **Vesper is freed: the Reveal** |
| 7 | **The Anima Vats** | The harvesting works under the Spire: vats of teal Anima, Blight crystal, the Prism's icy light at the very end | She goes down to end it; the Alchemist |
| — | **The Sovereign Prism** | The last level and the ending | |
| — | **The Lost Keep** | The Ironbark Guard's fortress: the first place lost, the last place taken back | Retaken in the epilogue [planned] |

All [planned] except the names, Kindreds and bosses, which are canon.

### Region 1, Flooded Sanctuary
The names follow the creative lead's painted region map: region 1 is
**Flooded Sanctuary**, level 1-1 is **Lantern Refuge**. The levels: 1-1
Lantern Refuge, 1-2 The Gate Road, 1-3 The Ruin Gardens, 1-4 The Aqueduct,
1-5 The Lantern Clearing, 1-S The Hollow Oak (the secret level), 1-B The
Keep Gate. The **Guard's banners** stand along the road as checkpoints.

**[open]** The region's look: its levels are bright day, its painted map
is dusk; 1-2 wears the Flooded Sanctuary stone. Which look 1-3 onward take
is undecided.

### Region 2, the Hearth-Plains [planned]
- **Foreshadowing:** carts left loaded in the road; mills turning with no
  one at the brake, or standing still where a miller was taken; silk on
  the fences.
- **2-5, The Harvest Run:** a **Carapax reaper**, the Vexari's harvest
  machine, comes through the wheat behind her. The first sight of what is
  taking people: *"they harvest more than wheat."*
- **2-B, the Silk Mother:** a quiet weaving barn; cocoons hang in rows,
  the missing; her loom's threads hum. Beaten, **her great cocoon opens and
  the Ironbark smith steps out.** Then the plains' people walk into the
  Refuge, and the Rootling kitchen opens.

### The Reveal
Freeing Vesper triggers the forbidden memory-meld: everyone sees that the
"Shepherds" are harvesters. The survivors rename the hub the **Memory-Meld
Refuge** (it is only "the Refuge" until then, so the name doesn't give the
twist away). Memory-meld is a story beat here, not one of Kleo's powers.
[planned]

### The regions Lost Keep had and this game doesn't
**The Deep-Glow Caverns, the Tide-Weaver coast and Morvath's Observatory**
have no boss among the seven, so they are kept for later: a bonus region,
DLC or a sequel (or, if the knots proposal holds, the Observatory after the
ending). [planned]

## 7. Kleo's gifts

Each boss's defeat gives Kleo a gift, and each is born where the story
puts it. [planned]

| After | Gift | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| (from the start) | **Anima Spark** | Her own teal Anima: a bolt (built, as a power-up) |
| Region 1 | **Ironbark Ward** | Her captain's shield, taken from the fallen warden |
| Region 2 | **Barkgrip** | Made by the Ironbark smith freed from the Silk Mother's great cocoon |
| Region 3 | **Emberwick** | The Ash-Walkers' fire-craft |
| Region 4 | **Wing-Cloak** | The Reaper's stolen Anima flows back into her and wakes her dormant wings |
| Region 5 | **Thorn-Line** | Morvath's gift at the Foundry's broken gate |
| Region 6 | **Anima Siphon** | Vexari technology, taken from a Shepherd: the same technology that caused the disaster |

**[open]** The creative lead changed abilities into **power-ups, as
Mario's** (found in a level, one held at a time, a hit costs the power
instead of a flame), and the newer power-up list doesn't match this table:
it gives Emberwick for region 2 and adds **Morvath's Star** for region 3.
Where each gift comes from in the story should follow whichever list is
kept. Barkgrip or Emberwick from the Silk Mother is an open question in
region 2's plan.

## 8. The Refuge

The hub between regions, in the forest edge **east of the Lost Keep**. It
starts nearly empty, with a single campfire. Every rescue adds someone,
and stalls open as people arrive. **Early on the Kindreds keep to their
own corners and argue; as rescues mount they share fires and songs:** the
unity the Vexari fear is something the player builds. Morvath, Sora and
Vesper appear here between regions; this is where the story is told.
[planned]

| Stall | Run by |
|---|---|
| The healer | Lantern flames |
| The Rootling kitchen | A meal before a level |
| The map-maker | What is still missing in each level |
| The tailor | Kleo's hat and long coat, cloak linings (never red) |
| The builders | Homes and gardens: the Refuge visibly grows |
| The archivist | Concept art, music, **the Alchemist's notes** |

Not built yet. After the Reveal it becomes **the Memory-Meld Refuge**.

## 9. Creatures

- **The Blight's creatures** are animals mutated by it. The creative lead's
  roster: bat, hood knife, skull shaman, cap crawler, sack thief,
  spineback, stone golem, moss blob, spiked toad, rat, gourd chomper,
  chained wraith (`design/enemies.md`; their names are working names).
- **The hooded figures** (hood knife, skull shaman, sack thief, chained
  wraith) must never read as Rootlings: they are cloth and bone animated
  by the Blight, empty hoods with a glowing eye, not people. [proposed]
- **The Mossback**, a gentle grazer, came over from Lost Keep.
- **The Carapax**: shell-forged automatons, from the reaper in the wheat
  (region 2) to the Foundry's armour (region 5).
